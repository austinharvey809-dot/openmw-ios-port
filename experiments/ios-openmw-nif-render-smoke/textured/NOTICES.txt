G1C adds a statically registered TGA reader from the unchanged pinned OSG tree.
Preserve the existing OpenMW, OSG, OpenThreads, Bullet and ANGLE notices shipped
by the G1B resource preparation. The TGA source carries this additional notice:

Follows is code extracted from the simage library. Original Authors:

Peder Blekken <pederb@sim.no>

The new ESM/BSA/NIF/TGA inputs are self-authored synthetic test assets generated
by tools/fixtures.py. They contain no extracted Bethesda game content.
