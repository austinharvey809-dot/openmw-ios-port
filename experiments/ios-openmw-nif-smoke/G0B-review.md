## Bullet dependency and licensing boundary

The parser's decoding path uses OSG math data types, not the OSG scene graph or ANGLE. `components/nif/extra.cpp` calls non-inline `osg::Quat::makeRotate`, so the pinned OSG core library is a real host-link requirement; header-only math is insufficient. The unchanged `components/nif/node.cpp` also compiles collision-shape methods, so it needs real Bullet headers and `BulletCollision`/`LinearMath` at link time even when G0B does not call collision methods. OpenMW's own CMake requires Bullet at least 2.87 and double precision; its fallback source is version 3.25 with the exact SHA-512 above. The reviewed helper follows that existing source pin and makes no system-Bullet assumption.

The Bullet archive is absent locally and was not downloaded. Its **actual license text and any included third-party notices could not be inspected offline**. The CI helper requires and preserves license files; before any redistribution of Bullet binaries or source, a human must inspect those files and satisfy their terms. The workflow artifact contains license/provenance evidence and logs, not Bullet source or game data. An archive without license files fails.
