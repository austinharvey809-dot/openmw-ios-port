## Review focus

- Real loader/source hashes and image failures cannot degrade silently into
  successful fallback meshes or warning textures.
- Plugin RefNums, moved leases, deletion and LTEX palettes preserve genuine
  load-order identities; known excluded classes have explicit counters.
- Area lifetime protects LAND/Bullet ownership; failed async loads roll back,
  and only complete scenes attach on the render thread.
- GLES adaptation retains upstream shading/blending math with explicit unsupported
  profiles. Source ESSL success does not establish driver or visual success.
- Existing app identity and Documents/Model two-file import remain compatible;
  optional ordered private manifests enable expansion/mod resource layering.
- One actual OSG/ANGLE provider and double Bullet ABI; no old mesh bridge in
  the G4 app. Accepted regression harnesses remain separate.
